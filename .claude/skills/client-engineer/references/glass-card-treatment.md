# Glass card treatment

Read this only when a task requests a glass, frosted, or translucent card.

The visual target is a light translucent panel with an 80% light pass, zero
refraction, depth 100, dispersion 100, frost 25 and splay 0. Godot approximates
unsupported refraction, depth and dispersion with a translucent white fill, soft
screen-space frost or blur when practical, a white edge, rounded corners and a
restrained shadow.
